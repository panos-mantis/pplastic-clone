Rub rails

Protective gunwale and hull rails that absorb impact and guard against abrasion.

Our rub rails shield hulls, decks and docks from knocks and wear. Extruded in durable PVC, they combine impact resistance with a clean, finished look.

Available in a range of profiles and colours — contact us with your requirement.

  • A range of profiles and colours
  • Supplied in coils or cut lengths
  • Custom sections to your sample or drawing
